Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 15, 2026

Welcome to EventCircle. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of personal information that is received and collected by EventCircle and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect or receive the following types of information when you use EventCircle:

  • Personal Information: Includes name, email address, and profile details provided during account creation.
  • Event Data: Information regarding events you create or join, including titles, descriptions, and participant lists.
  • Transaction Data: Details about contributions or payments made within the app.
  • Technical Data: Device information and usage statistics to improve application performance.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain EventCircle features.
  • Coordinate event logistics and communications between participants.
  • Processing payments and managing financial transparency.
  • Monitor usage patterns to improve user experience.

3. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information. However, please note that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We leverage secure cloud providers (Google Firebase) for robust infrastructure security.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services that collect information used to identify you, including:

  • Google Analytics / Firebase Services
  • Payment Processors (e.g., Razorpay)

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal data within the app settings. If you wish to permanently delete your account and all associated data, you can do so through the app or by contacting us.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.
